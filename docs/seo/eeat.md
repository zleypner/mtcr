# E-E-A-T Strategy

E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

## Why E-E-A-T Matters for Medical Content

Google holds medical content (YMYL - Your Money Your Life) to higher standards. Poor E-E-A-T signals can prevent ranking regardless of other SEO factors.

## Experience

### Demonstrating Experience

1. **Patient Stories**
   - Real testimonials from verified patients
   - Before/after documentation (where appropriate)
   - Video testimonials

2. **First-Hand Knowledge**
   - Original photography of facilities
   - Staff interviews
   - Procedure documentation

3. **User-Generated Content**
   - Verified reviews
   - Q&A sections
   - Community forums (future)

## Expertise

### Medical Expertise Signals

1. **Medical Review Process**
   - All medical content reviewed by licensed physicians
   - Display "Medically Reviewed By" with credentials
   - Include review date

2. **Author Credentials**
   ```markdown
   Written by: Editorial Team
   Medical Review: Dr. Carlos Rodríguez, MD, Board Certified Orthopedic Surgeon
   Last Reviewed: June 2024
   ```

3. **Expert Contributors**
   - Guest articles from Costa Rican physicians
   - Interviews with specialists
   - Medical advisory board (future)

### Content Expertise

1. **Comprehensive Coverage**
   - Answer all related questions
   - Include technical details
   - Cite medical sources

2. **Accuracy**
   - Fact-check all claims
   - Update statistics regularly
   - Correct errors promptly

## Authoritativeness

### Building Authority

1. **Backlink Profile**
   - Earn links from medical institutions
   - Featured in health publications
   - Cited by other medical tourism sites

2. **Mentions & Citations**
   - Press coverage
   - Industry recognition
   - Academic citations

3. **Industry Presence**
   - Medical tourism associations
   - Healthcare conferences
   - Professional partnerships

### On-Site Authority Signals

1. **About Page**
   - Company mission and values
   - Team credentials
   - Company history
   - Physical address

2. **Contact Information**
   - Phone number
   - Email
   - Physical location
   - Response time commitment

## Trustworthiness

### Trust Signals

1. **Transparency**
   - Clear pricing information
   - Honest about limitations
   - Disclose affiliate relationships
   - Editorial independence statement

2. **Security**
   - HTTPS everywhere
   - Privacy policy
   - Data protection compliance
   - Secure contact forms

3. **Accuracy**
   - Cite sources
   - Link to official sources
   - Update outdated content
   - Correction policy

4. **User Protection**
   - No misleading claims
   - Medical disclaimers
   - Emergency contact information
   - Complaint process

### Medical Disclaimers

Include on all medical content:

```markdown
**Medical Disclaimer:** This information is for educational purposes only
and does not constitute medical advice. Consult with qualified healthcare
providers before making medical decisions. Individual results may vary.
```
