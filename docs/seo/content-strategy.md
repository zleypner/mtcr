## Content Calendar

Maintain rolling 3-month content calendar:
- Week 1-2: Pillar content
- Week 3: Supporting cluster content
- Week 4: Blog posts and updates

## Content Quality Standards

Every piece of content must:
- Answer the search intent completely
- Include original insights or data
- Link to 3+ internal pages
- Include relevant structured data
- Be medically reviewed (medical content)
- Meet E-E-A-T requirements
