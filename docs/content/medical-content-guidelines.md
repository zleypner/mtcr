# Medical Content Guidelines

## Purpose

Medical content on this site must be:
- Accurate and evidence-based
- Helpful to patients making decisions
- Compliant with health content standards
- Trustworthy and transparent

## YMYL Compliance

Medical content is classified as "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) by Google. This means:
- Higher quality standards required
- E-E-A-T signals critically important
- Misinformation can cause real harm
- Content must be medically reviewed

## Content Categories

### Category A: General Information
Information available from public health sources
- What is a procedure
- General recovery expectations
- Costa Rica healthcare overview

**Requirements:**
- Link to authoritative sources
- Include general disclaimer

### Category B: Treatment Details
Specific procedural information
- How procedures work
- Typical outcomes
- Risk factors

**Requirements:**
- Medical review required
- Cite medical literature
- Include specific disclaimer
- Note individual variation

### Category C: Medical Advice Adjacent
Content that approaches medical advice
- Treatment comparisons
- Provider recommendations
- Cost-benefit analyses

**Requirements:**
- Medical professional review
- Strong disclaimers
- Emphasize consultation need
- No absolute claims

## Required Elements

### Every Medical Page

1. **Medical Review Badge**
```
Medically reviewed by [Name], [Credentials]
Last reviewed: [Date]
```

2. **Medical Disclaimer**
```
This information is for educational purposes only and does not
replace professional medical advice. Consult with a qualified
healthcare provider before making medical decisions.
```

3. **Sources Section**
List all medical sources cited

4. **Last Updated Date**
Prominently displayed

### Treatment Pages

Additional requirements:
- Procedure overview
- Candidacy information
- Risk acknowledgment
- Recovery timeline (ranges, not guarantees)
- Cost information (ranges with disclaimers)

## Language Guidelines

### Use
- "May help with" not "will cure"
- "Typically" not "always"
- "Many patients experience" not "you will experience"
- "Consult your doctor" for any decision

### Avoid
- Absolute claims ("guaranteed results")
- Fear-mongering language
- Unsubstantiated statistics
- Testimonials as medical evidence
- Before/after without context

## Accuracy Standards

### Verification Process

1. Claim is made
2. Source is identified
3. Source credibility verified
4. Claim checked against source
5. Medical reviewer confirms

### Acceptable Sources

**Tier 1 (Preferred):**
- Peer-reviewed medical journals
- Medical associations (AMA, specialty boards)
- Government health agencies (WHO, CDC, NIH)
- Accreditation bodies (JCI)

**Tier 2 (Acceptable):**
- Hospital/clinic published research
- Medical textbooks (current editions)
- Recognized medical news outlets

**Tier 3 (Limited Use):**
- News articles (link to original source)
- Industry publications
- Expert interviews

**Not Acceptable:**
- Wikipedia (use underlying sources)
- Personal blogs
- Outdated sources (>5 years for most topics)
- Anonymous sources
- Social media

## Sensitive Topics

### Cosmetic Procedures
- No body shaming
- Acknowledge psychological aspects
- Emphasize informed consent
- Include realistic expectations

### Fertility Treatments
- Sensitive to emotional aspects
- Acknowledge success rate limitations
- No guarantees
- Support resources mentioned

### Weight Loss Procedures
- No judgment language
- Medical criteria clearly stated
- Long-term lifestyle mentioned
- Risks prominently displayed

## Update Requirements

| Content Type | Review Frequency |
|--------------|------------------|
| Treatment pages | Every 12 months |
| Provider profiles | Every 6 months |
| Statistics | When new data available |
| Medical guidelines | When guidelines change |

## Red Flags (Never Publish)

- Claims of 100% success rates
- "Miracle" or "breakthrough" language
- Attacks on conventional medicine
- Unverified patient results
- Medical advice without consultation recommendation
- Outdated medical information
- Missing disclaimers
