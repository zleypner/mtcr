# Writing Guidelines

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Authoritative** but approachable
- **Informative** but not overwhelming
- **Reassuring** but honest
- **Professional** but warm

### Tone by Content Type

| Content Type | Tone |
|--------------|------|
| Medical guides | Professional, detailed, reassuring |
| Treatment pages | Informative, clear, factual |
| Patient stories | Warm, personal, inspiring |
| Blog posts | Conversational, engaging |
| FAQ | Direct, helpful, concise |

## Audience

Primary: US/Canada adults (35-65) considering medical procedures abroad

### What they need:
- Reassurance about safety and quality
- Clear cost information
- Practical planning details
- Credible medical information
- Real patient experiences

### What they fear:
- Unsafe conditions
- Communication barriers
- Hidden costs
- Being far from home
- Complications

## Writing Standards

### Clarity
- Write at 8th-grade reading level
- Use short sentences (15-20 words average)
- One idea per paragraph
- Active voice preferred

### Accuracy
- Verify all medical claims
- Cite sources for statistics
- Use current data only
- Avoid absolute claims

### Structure
- Lead with the most important information
- Use descriptive headings
- Include bullet points for lists
- Break up long sections

## SEO Writing

### Title Tags
- Primary keyword near start
- 50-60 characters
- Include brand for home/key pages
- Compelling and accurate

```
Good: "Dental Implants in Costa Rica: Costs, Clinics & Guide"
Bad: "Everything You Need to Know About Getting Dental Implants"
```

### Meta Descriptions
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Include call-to-action
- Summarize page value

```
Good: "Save 50-70% on dental implants in Costa Rica. Compare costs,
JCI-accredited clinics, and top dentists. Get your free quote today."
```

### Headings
- One H1 per page (the title)
- H2s for main sections
- H3s for subsections
- Include keywords naturally

### Body Content
- Primary keyword in first 100 words
- Related keywords throughout
- Natural language (no keyword stuffing)
- Answer search intent completely

## Medical Content Guidelines

### Accuracy Requirements
- Cite medical sources (journals, institutions)
- Use current medical terminology
- Have content reviewed by medical professional
- Update annually at minimum

### Required Disclaimers

Every medical page must include:

```markdown
**Medical Disclaimer:** This content is for informational purposes only
and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult with qualified
healthcare providers before making medical decisions.
```

### Sensitive Content
- No guarantee of outcomes
- Acknowledge risks honestly
- Recommend professional consultation
- Avoid fear-mongering

### Sources to Cite
- Peer-reviewed journals
- Medical associations
- Government health agencies
- Accreditation bodies
- **Not:** Wikipedia, random blogs, outdated sources

## Formatting Standards

### Numbers
- Spell out one through nine
- Use numerals for 10+
- Always use numerals for measurements, ages, money
- Use $ for USD (our primary currency)

### Dates
- Format: January 15, 2024
- Avoid relative dates ("last year", "recently")

### Links
- Descriptive anchor text
- Open external links in new tab
- Nofollow commercial external links
- Follow internal links

### Lists
- Use bullets for unordered lists
- Use numbers for sequential steps
- Keep list items parallel in structure
- Limit to 7 items when possible

## Content Length Guidelines

| Content Type | Target Length |
|--------------|---------------|
| Treatment page | 1,500-2,500 words |
| Provider profile | 800-1,200 words |
| Blog post | 1,000-2,000 words |
| Comprehensive guide | 3,000-5,000 words |
| FAQ answer | 100-300 words |
| Patient story | 500-1,000 words |

## Review Process

1. **Writer** creates draft
2. **Editor** reviews for style/clarity
3. **Medical reviewer** verifies accuracy (medical content)
4. **SEO review** ensures optimization
5. **Final approval** before publishing
