# Medical Tourism in Escazú, Costa Rica

Escazú is widely recognized as the premier upscale suburb of San José and the primary center for high-end private healthcare in Costa Rica. Known for its modern infrastructure, safety, luxury hotels, and high density of bilingual medical and dental offices, Escazú provides an ideal environment for international patients undergoing elective treatments and recovery.

## Why Escazú for Medical Travel?

### Concentration of Private Care

Escazú hosts some of the most advanced healthcare resources in Central America:

- **Hospital CIMA**: A leading private hospital in the region, offering full-scale emergency, ICU, and surgical suites.
- **State-of-the-Art Dental Centers**: Highly specialized clinics for implantology, cosmetic veneers, and full-mouth rehabilitation.
- **Accredited Surgical Suites**: Licensed plastic and reconstructive surgery centers operating under strict Ministry of Health oversight.

### Safe, Walkable Suburb

For patients recovering from surgery, environment is everything:

- **Low-density residential feel** combined with modern commercial plazas.
- **Highly secure neighborhoods** popular with international residents and diplomats.
- **Walkable access** to pharmacies, supermarkets, and cafes in districts like Trejos Montealegre.

### Hospitality and Lodging

Escazú offers a wide range of recovery-friendly accommodations:

- **Luxury and business hotels** equipped with accessible rooms and elevator access.
- **Extended-stay apartments** featuring full kitchens for family members.
- **Proximity to medical retreats** providing on-site nursing assistance and tailored meal planning.

## Key Districts & Areas

### San Rafael de Escazú

The commercial heart of the district:
- Runs along the main avenue (Calle Vieja).
- High density of dental offices, pharmacies, and specialty clinics.
- Walking distance to shopping centers like Plaza Laureles.

### Guachipelín

A modern development area:
- Located north of the main highway.
- Home to outpatient clinics and professional offices.
- Offers modern, high-rise lodging options.

### Trejos Montealegre

A calm residential pocket:
- Directly adjacent to Hospital CIMA.
- Peaceful, tree-lined streets ideal for light post-operative walks.
- Houses multiple boutique hotels and short-term rentals catering to medical travelers.

## General Travel Logistics

- **Airport Access**: Located approximately 18 km (about 20-30 minutes depending on traffic) from Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO).
- **Transport**: Private medical shuttles, registered taxis (red cars), and rideshare services are widely available.
- **Language**: English is spoken by the vast majority of medical professionals, hotel staff, and in commercial establishments throughout Escazú.
