# Medical Tourism in San José, Costa Rica

San José, the capital of Costa Rica, serves as the primary hub for medical tourism in the country. Home to the nation's best hospitals, most renowned specialists, and excellent infrastructure, San José offers international patients a comprehensive medical destination with all the conveniences of a modern capital city.

## Why San José for Medical Treatment?

### Medical Hub of Costa Rica

San José concentrates the best healthcare resources:

- **3 JCI-accredited hospitals** within the metro area
- **Largest concentration of specialists** in the country
- **Advanced medical technology** and equipment
- **International patient departments** with coordinators

### Accessibility

Getting to San José is straightforward:

- **Direct flights** from major US cities (Miami, Houston, Dallas, Los Angeles)
- **3-hour flight** from Miami
- **Modern airport** with efficient customs
- **Reliable ground transportation** to medical facilities

### Infrastructure for Recovery

The city offers excellent amenities:

- **Recovery hotels** near hospitals
- **Pharmacies** with most medications available
- **Modern shopping centers** and restaurants
- **Reliable internet and communications**

## Medical Districts

### Escazú

The premier area for medical tourism:

- Upscale suburb west of San José
- Home to CIMA Hospital (JCI-accredited)
- Luxury hotels and apartments
- High-end restaurants and shopping
- Safe, walkable neighborhoods
- 20 minutes from airport

### Santa Ana

Modern development with medical facilities:

- Growing medical district
- Dental clinics and cosmetic surgery centers
- Mid-range to luxury accommodations
- Near Escazú amenities
- 25 minutes from airport

### La Sabana

Central location near major hospitals:

- Adjacent to Clínica Bíblica (JCI-accredited)
- Urban setting with parks
- Variety of hotels and Airbnbs
- Good public transportation
- Walking distance to amenities

### Calle Blancos

Eastern medical district:

- Home to Hospital CIMA San José
- Residential feel
- More affordable options
- Easy access to downtown

## Major Hospitals

### Hospital CIMA San José

**JCI Accredited** | Escazú

Part of the international CIMA health network:

- 87 beds, 6 operating rooms
- Full range of specialties
- International patient services
- Affiliated recovery suites
- English-speaking staff

**Specialties:** Cardiac surgery, orthopedics, oncology, bariatric surgery

### Clínica Bíblica

**JCI Accredited** | La Sabana

Oldest and most recognized private hospital:

- Established 1929
- 120+ beds
- Comprehensive medical services
- Dedicated international department
- Multiple locations

**Specialties:** Cardiac care, oncology, general surgery, neurology

### Hospital Clínica Católica

**JCI Accredited** | Guadalupe

Part of the national healthcare network:

- Full-service hospital
- Emergency services
- Specialized surgical suites
- Growing international program

**Specialties:** Orthopedics, gastroenterology, general surgery

## Getting Around

### Airport Transfers

- **Distance from SJO:** 17 km (30-45 minutes)
- **Hospital transportation:** Most offer airport pickup
- **Private services:** Reliable and affordable
- **Ride-sharing:** Uber operates in San José

### Local Transportation

- **Uber:** Widely available and affordable
- **Taxis:** Official red taxis are safe
- **Hospital shuttles:** Between facilities and hotels
- **Walking:** Possible in medical districts

## Practical Information

### Climate

- **Temperature:** 20-26°C (68-79°F) year-round
- **Dry season:** December-April
- **Rainy season:** May-November (afternoon showers)
- **Altitude:** 1,172 m (3,845 ft) - mild temperatures

### What to Expect

- **English:** Widely spoken in medical facilities
- **Currency:** Costa Rican Colón (USD accepted many places)
- **Pharmacies:** Well-stocked, many open 24/7
- **Safety:** Generally safe in medical districts

## Tips for Medical Travelers

### Before Your Trip

- Confirm all appointments in writing
- Arrange airport pickup with your clinic
- Book accommodation near your medical facility
- Bring copies of medical records

### During Your Stay

- Keep your coordinator's contact information handy
- Follow all pre and post-operative instructions
- Stay hydrated (high altitude can be dehydrating)
- Allow extra time between appointments

### After Your Procedure

- Attend all follow-up appointments
- Don't rush back to the airport
- Keep receipts for insurance claims
- Follow up with your doctor remotely

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far are hospitals from the airport?

Most major hospitals are 20-40 minutes from Juan Santamaría International Airport, depending on traffic.

### Is San José safe for medical tourists?

San José's medical districts (Escazú, Santa Ana, La Sabana) are generally safe. Use normal precautions and stick to recommended areas.

### What's the best area to stay?

Escazú is the most popular for medical tourists due to its proximity to CIMA Hospital, safety, and amenities. La Sabana is ideal for patients at Clínica Bíblica.

### Should I bring medication from home?

Bring essential medications, but most are available locally. Your clinic can advise on what's available in Costa Rica.
