## What Are Dental Veneers?

Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of teeth. They transform the appearance of your smile by improving the color, shape, size, and alignment of teeth.

### Benefits of Veneers

- **Instant transformation** - Dramatic improvement in days
- **Natural appearance** - Custom-matched to your features
- **Stain resistant** - Porcelain resists discoloration
- **Durable** - Long-lasting with proper care
- **Minimally invasive** - Less tooth reduction than crowns

## Types of Veneers

### Porcelain Veneers

The gold standard for smile makeovers:

**Pros:**
- Most natural appearance
- Highly stain-resistant
- Last 10-15+ years
- Custom shade matching
- Strong and durable

**Cons:**
- Higher cost (still 50-60% less than USA)
- Requires two visits
- Irreversible (tooth preparation required)

**Cost in Costa Rica:** $450-650 per veneer
**USA Cost:** $1,000-2,500 per veneer

### E.max Veneers

Premium lithium disilicate porcelain:

- Stronger than traditional porcelain
- Excellent translucency
- Minimal tooth preparation possible
- Premium option at Costa Rica prices

**Cost in Costa Rica:** $500-700 per veneer

### Composite Veneers

More affordable option:

**Pros:**
- Same-day procedure
- Less expensive
- Reversible (less tooth preparation)
- Easy to repair

**Cons:**
- Less durable (5-7 years)
- More prone to staining
- May require more maintenance

**Cost in Costa Rica:** $250-400 per veneer

### No-Prep Veneers (Lumineers-style)

Ultra-thin veneers requiring minimal preparation:

- No or minimal tooth reduction
- Potentially reversible
- Good for minor corrections
- Not suitable for all cases

**Cost in Costa Rica:** $450-650 per veneer

## The Procedure

### Consultation and Design

**Virtual Consultation:**
- Share photos of your current smile
- Discuss goals and expectations
- Receive preliminary recommendations
- Digital smile preview (some clinics)

**In-Person Assessment:**
- Comprehensive oral examination
- Digital imaging and X-rays
- Shade selection
- Treatment plan finalization

### Visit 1: Preparation

**Preparation Day (2-3 hours):**

1. Local anesthesia administered
2. Minimal enamel removed (0.3-0.5mm)
3. Digital impressions taken
4. Temporary veneers placed
5. Impressions sent to lab

**What to Expect:**
- Teeth may feel different initially
- Temporary veneers protect teeth
- Some sensitivity normal
- Soft diet recommended

### Between Visits

- Duration: 3-7 days typically
- In-house labs may be faster
- Temporary veneers are functional
- Continue normal oral hygiene

### Visit 2: Placement

**Bonding Day (2-3 hours):**

1. Temporary veneers removed
2. Permanent veneers tried in
3. Adjustments made as needed
4. Veneers permanently bonded
5. Final polishing and adjustments
6. Care instructions provided

**Immediate Results:**
- Walk out with your new smile
- Natural appearance from day one
- Minimal adjustment period
- Full function immediately

## Who Is a Good Candidate?

### Ideal For

- **Discolored teeth** that don't respond to whitening
- **Chipped or worn teeth**
- **Minor misalignment** or gaps
- **Irregularly shaped teeth**
- **Smile makeover** desires

### Requirements

- Healthy teeth and gums
- Adequate enamel for bonding
- No severe grinding (or willing to wear night guard)
- Realistic expectations
- Commitment to oral hygiene

### Not Ideal For

- Severe tooth decay (address first)
- Advanced gum disease (treat first)
- Heavy teeth grinding without protection
- Very small or weak teeth
- Major bite issues (orthodontics may be better)

## Recovery and Aftercare

### Immediate Recovery (Days 1-2)

- Minor sensitivity possible
- Gums may be slightly tender
- Eat soft foods initially
- Avoid very hot/cold foods

### Long-Term Care

**To Maximize Veneer Lifespan:**

- Maintain excellent oral hygiene
- Brush and floss daily
- Regular dental check-ups
- Wear night guard if you grind
- Avoid biting hard objects
- Limit staining beverages

**Avoid:**
- Biting nails or pens
- Opening packages with teeth
- Ice chewing
- Excessive coffee/red wine (composite veneers)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many veneers do I need?

A typical smile makeover covers 6-10 upper teeth (the "smile zone"). A full transformation may include 16-20 teeth. Your dentist will recommend based on your goals.

### Do veneers look natural?

Quality porcelain veneers are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. Skilled cosmetic dentists match the shade, translucency, and shape to your facial features.

### Are veneers permanent?

Porcelain veneers last 10-15+ years with proper care. They will eventually need replacement. Some tooth preparation is irreversible, meaning you'll always need some form of restoration.

### Can I see what my smile will look like before?

Many Costa Rican clinics offer digital smile design, showing you a preview of your potential results before treatment begins.

### What if I don't like the results?

Reputable clinics work with you until you're satisfied. Choose a dentist who offers adjustments and has a clear revision policy.

### Will I need time off work?

Most patients can return to work the day after their final appointment. Budget 1-2 days for any residual sensitivity.
