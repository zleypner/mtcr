For many people struggling with weight loss, traditional bariatric surgery feels like a significant step—one that involves incisions, hospitalization, and permanent changes to the digestive system. Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty (ESG) offers an alternative: an effective weight loss procedure without the need for open surgery or incisions.

Costa Rica has emerged as a leading destination for ESG procedures, combining experienced specialists, modern technology, and significant cost savings compared to the United States and other countries.

## What Is Endoscopic Sleeve Gastroplasty?

Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty, also known as endoscopic gastric sleeve or ESG, is a minimally invasive procedure for weight loss that requires **no open surgery and no incisions**.

The procedure is performed by inserting an endoscope through the mouth into the stomach. Using specialized suturing tools attached to the endoscope, the gastroenterologist places internal sutures that reduce the stomach's size and shape, limiting the amount of food you can comfortably consume.

Unlike traditional gastric sleeve surgery, ESG:
- Leaves no scars on your abdomen
- Requires no cutting or stapling of stomach tissue
- Is performed entirely through the mouth
- Typically allows same-day discharge or one night of observation

## How Does ESG Work?

The procedure reduces your stomach to approximately **70-80% smaller** than its original size. This achieves two important effects:

1. **You feel full faster**: With a smaller stomach capacity, smaller portions create a satisfying feeling of fullness

2. **Food stays longer in the stomach**: The modified stomach shape slows digestion, prolonging the sensation of satiety between meals

The result is that you naturally eat less without feeling constantly hungry, which facilitates sustained weight loss over time.

## Who Is a Candidate for ESG?

Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty may be a suitable option if you:

- **Have a BMI between 30 and 40**: ESG is designed for patients with moderate obesity who may not qualify for or prefer to avoid traditional bariatric surgery
- **Have tried diet and exercise without lasting success**: Candidates have typically made serious efforts at lifestyle-based weight loss
- **Prefer to avoid traditional surgery**: Many patients are drawn to the non-surgical nature of ESG
- **Are committed to lifestyle changes**: ESG works best as part of a comprehensive weight management program including dietary changes and physical activity
- **Have no medical contraindications**: Certain conditions may make ESG unsuitable; a thorough evaluation determines candidacy

During your consultation, the medical team will evaluate your individual case to determine if this procedure is right for you.

## Advantages Over Traditional Bariatric Surgery

ESG offers several important benefits compared to surgical weight loss procedures:

### No Incisions or Scars
Everything is done through the mouth. There are no cuts in the abdomen, no staples, and no visible scars.

### Faster Recovery
Most patients can return to normal activities within a few days, compared to weeks for traditional surgery.

### Outpatient Procedure
Many patients go home the same day, without the need for hospitalization. Some may stay one night for observation.

### Reversible
Unlike surgical procedures that permanently remove or reroute parts of the digestive system, the sutures placed during ESG can potentially be adjusted or removed if needed.

### Lower Risk Profile
With no surgical incisions, the risk of complications such as infection, hernia, or wound problems is significantly reduced compared to traditional bariatric surgery.

### Fewer Nutritional Deficiencies
Because ESG doesn't alter nutrient absorption (as some bariatric surgeries do), patients typically experience fewer vitamin and mineral deficiencies.

## How Much Weight Can I Expect to Lose?

Research shows that patients can typically lose between **15% and 20% of their total body weight** within the first 12 months after ESG.

For example:
- A person weighing 220 lbs (100 kg) might lose 33-44 lbs (15-20 kg)
- A person weighing 250 lbs (113 kg) might lose 37-50 lbs (17-23 kg)

The best results are achieved when patients combine the procedure with:
- **Dietary modifications**: Working with a nutritionist to establish healthy eating patterns
- **Regular physical activity**: Incorporating exercise appropriate to your fitness level
- **Ongoing medical follow-up**: Regular check-ins to monitor progress and address challenges
- **Behavioral support**: Addressing the psychological aspects of eating and weight management

## What Are the Risks?

Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty has a favorable safety profile. The most common side effects are temporary and typically resolve within the first few days:

- **Nausea**: Common in the first 24-72 hours as your body adjusts
- **Mild abdominal discomfort**: Usually manageable with prescribed medications
- **Feeling of fullness**: Even with small amounts of food initially
- **Sore throat**: From the endoscope passage, resolving within a day or two

Serious complications are rare, especially when the procedure is performed by an experienced gastroenterologist trained in bariatric endoscopy.

## How Do I Prepare for ESG?

Preparation for endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty includes:

### Before the Procedure
- **Special diet**: You'll follow a specific pre-procedure diet for several days to prepare your body
- **Fasting**: Nothing to eat or drink after midnight before the procedure
- **Medication review**: Inform your doctor about all medications you take; some may need to be adjusted
- **Arrange transportation**: You'll need someone to drive you home after the procedure
- **Pre-operative tests**: Blood work and other evaluations to ensure you're ready for the procedure

Your medical team will provide detailed, personalized instructions during your consultation.

## What Happens After ESG?

Recovery follows a structured progression to allow your stomach to heal properly:

### First Week: Liquid Diet
You'll consume only clear liquids and protein shakes. This allows the sutures to set and your stomach to begin healing.

### Second Week: Soft Foods
Gradually introduce soft, pureed foods. Focus on protein-rich options.

### Third Week and Beyond: Gradual Progression
Slowly reintroduce solid foods, starting with soft proteins and cooked vegetables. Your nutritionist will guide the transition.

### Ongoing Follow-Up
Regular appointments monitor your weight loss progress, nutritional status, and overall health. These check-ins are crucial for long-term success.

## ESG vs. Other Weight Loss Options

### ESG vs. Gastric Sleeve Surgery
- **Gastric sleeve** permanently removes about 80% of the stomach through surgical incisions
- **ESG** reduces stomach size through internal sutures without any incisions
- Gastric sleeve typically results in greater weight loss but carries higher surgical risks
- ESG offers a middle ground between diet/exercise and major surgery

### ESG vs. Gastric Balloon
- **Gastric balloon** is a temporary device placed in the stomach for 6-12 months
- **ESG** creates longer-lasting stomach reduction
- ESG typically produces greater and more sustained weight loss than balloon
- Both are non-surgical options

### ESG vs. Diet and Exercise Alone
- For patients with BMI 30-40 who haven't succeeded with lifestyle changes alone, ESG provides a physical tool to support their efforts
- ESG works best combined with diet and exercise, not as a replacement

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does the ESG procedure take?

The procedure typically takes 60-90 minutes. Plan for several hours total including preparation and recovery.

### Is ESG painful?

The procedure is performed under sedation or general anesthesia, so you won't feel anything during it. Post-procedure discomfort is usually mild and manageable with medication.

### How soon can I return to work?

Most patients return to desk work within 3-5 days. Physical labor may require 1-2 weeks off.

### Will I need to take vitamins after ESG?

Unlike some bariatric surgeries, ESG doesn't typically cause significant nutritional deficiencies. However, your doctor may recommend a multivitamin as part of your overall health plan.

### Can the procedure be reversed?

The sutures can potentially be removed or revised if needed, unlike permanent surgical procedures. However, ESG is intended as a long-term weight loss solution.

### How long do the results last?

With proper dietary habits and lifestyle changes, weight loss can be maintained long-term. Some patients may benefit from a touch-up procedure after several years.

### Is ESG covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by insurance plan and country. Many patients find that even paying out-of-pocket in Costa Rica is more affordable than paying a deductible for surgery at home.
